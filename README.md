# Sangkyoon Nam

I build backend platforms and the teams behind them.

I spent twelve years at NBT, where I led AdiSON development and ran the engineering organization through the company's KOSDAQ listing. AdiSON is Korea's largest rewarded-ad offerwall by market share, integrated into apps including Naver Webtoon, Naver Pay, KakaoTalk, Toss and Baemin. At Datarize, I built the foundation platform (API gateway, identity and core services) and event-triggered messaging.

I'm bringing lessons from years of running large-scale, real-time backends to AI agents, so interrupted runs can resume and failures can be traced. In open source, I contribute to [Apache Flink](https://github.com/search?q=is%3Apr+author%3Asangkyoonnam+repo%3Aapache%2Fflink&type=pullrequests), primarily through its [Flink Agents](https://github.com/search?q=is%3Apr+author%3Asangkyoonnam+repo%3Aapache%2Fflink-agents&type=pullrequests) subproject, where I work on durable execution and model routing. I also work on GenAI instrumentation and semantic conventions in [OpenTelemetry](https://github.com/search?q=is%3Apr+author%3Asangkyoonnam+org%3Aopen-telemetry&type=pullrequests), and on the Java SDK and agent framework integrations (LangGraph, Google ADK) for [Temporal](https://github.com/search?q=is%3Apr+author%3Asangkyoonnam+org%3Atemporalio&type=pullrequests).
